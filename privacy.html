Privacy Policy — Effective: October 2026 · Soupyy v1

Your data stays on your device

Soupyy stores everything you create — your recipes, photos, cookbooks, follows, likes, and comments — locally on this device only. We cannot see, access, or share any of it. The one exception is signing in with Google, described below.

What we store locally

Your email address and, if you sign in with a password, a salted hashed version of it (never the password itself); your profile name, handle, bio, and photos; the recipes, images, cookbooks, and activity you create in the app; and your theme preference.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo with Soupyy to create or sign in to your local account. This happens directly between your device and Google — Soupyy has no server to relay it through, and nothing beyond that initial sign-in exchange is sent to Google afterward. See Google's own privacy policy for how Google handles its side of this.

Camera and photos

Soupyy asks for camera and photo-library permission only when you attach photos to a recipe or your profile. Selected photos are referenced from your device storage and are never transmitted anywhere.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Soupyy contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking. The only network requests it makes are the ones required for optional Google Sign-In — everything else stays on your device.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Children

Soupyy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly store their information.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be shown here and in the app. Questions? Contact the developer at rautsuprit@gmail.com.
